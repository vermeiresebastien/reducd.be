Quad Sequencer

Four synths on one clock. Fourteen scale degrees (2×7) across sixteen steps, shared key/mode, global transpose, per-synth pattern shift, and distinct FX chains.

Key and mode rebuild the 14-note lanes for every synth. Semitone/octave moves the whole project pitch. Shift moves each synth’s 16-step pattern forward or backward without changing the other synths.
